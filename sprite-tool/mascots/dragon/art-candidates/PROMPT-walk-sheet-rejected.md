## Prompt

Use case: sketch-to-render. Asset type: EXACTLY EIGHT animation sprite poses on ONE 4-column by 2-row sheet, landscape aspect 2:1. Image 1 is the approved orange dragon visual identity, including the corrected darker rear leg ending in yellow claws and a separate plain tail underside. Image 2 is the EDIT TARGET: an eight-pose approved walking mannequin sheet. Replace each mannequin with the dragon of Image 1 IN THAT MANNEQUIN'S EXACT POSE. Keep the exact 4x2 equal square cell layout, figure positions, scale, ground baselines, joint and foot placements of Image 2. Each cell represents a 112x112 logical sprite, enlarged using crisp square pixels. Order left to right on the top row then bottom row. Do not merely repeat Image 1 eight times. The orange near leg and blue far leg in Image 2 are pose instructions, not final skin colors: near leg bright orange; far leg darker burnt orange. First four poses near leg planted moving backward relative to the body while far leg folds and passes forward; second four poses far leg planted and near leg swings. Copy the eight distinct leg bends and feet heights from the corresponding mannequin. Keep head, face, blue eye, horns, wing, tail, torso shape and palette identical across the eight frames except the tiny prescribed body bob from Image 2. Preserve constant segment lengths and foot size, two legs only, claws attached only to feet or hands, never underneath tail. Draw dark outline separators wherever the rear leg crosses the tail. Reuse Image 1's charming face, yellow belly, orange skin and wings, yellow horns, pixel shading and thick dark outline. No captions, numbers, grids or dividers. Plain uniform navy #182238 background, no ground lines in final sheet, no cast shadows. Do not stretch or enlarge characters to fill their cells. Clean low-resolution pixel art, no gradients, blur or painterly texture. This is a cohesive sprite sheet with eight different phases of ONE right-facing walk cycle, not eight separate character designs.
